Our Legacy & Journey

Munna Catering Services has been at the forefront of creating culinary memories. From romantic royal weddings and sleek corporate seminars to intimate family house gatherings, we deliver bespoke tastes cooked with love.

2010 - The Humble Beginning

Founded by culinary enthusiasts with a vision to bring authentic Indian flavors and premium hospitality services directly to NCR households. We began with private dinners and intimate house caterings.

2016 - Tableware & setup Overhaul

Recognizing that presentation is half the meal, we expanded into dedicated crockery services, importing high-end Bone China tableware, building custom event decors, and creating buffet counters.

2020 - Crossing 1,000 Major Events

Celebrated a major milestone of catering over 1,000 wedding and corporate celebrations across major states in Northern India, refining processes to manage massive guest lists with consistency.

2026 - Modern Culinary Pioneers

Today, we integrate modern trends like molecular gastronomy counters, organic farm-to-table selections, and global cuisine menus with classical Indian culinary roots, delivering state-of-the-art experiences.
