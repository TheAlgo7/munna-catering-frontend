Frequently Asked Questions

Have queries about bookings, locations, or food? We have answers.

What is the minimum guest size required for booking?

+

For large services (such as setups, live counters, LED buffers), our minimum guest size is usually 50 guests. However, for exclusive sit-down chef table dining, we can cater to smaller premium gatherings from 20 guests onwards.

Do you offer customized menus for food restrictions?

+

Absolutely! We specialize in catering to gluten-free, vegan, nut-free, Jain-friendly, and other organic food requests. Our menu planner will craft a menu keeping all food alerts in mind.

Do you travel outside the local NCR area for catering?

+

Yes, Munna Caterers travels across India for premium wedding events. We have successfully catered to celebrations in Rajasthan, Himachal, Punjab, and Uttar Pradesh. Travel charges are added based on locations.

How far in advance should we secure our dates?

+

For prime wedding seasons (November to February), we recommend booking 4 to 6 months in advance. For corporate events or minor gatherings, 2 to 3 weeks ahead is usually sufficient to secure dates and select options.
